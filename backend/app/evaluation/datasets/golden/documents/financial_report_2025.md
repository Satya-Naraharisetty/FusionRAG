# Annual Financial & Operating Performance Report 2025

## Section 1: Executive Financial Summary
For Fiscal Year 2025 (FY2025 ending December 31, 2025), total consolidated GAAP revenue reached $142.5 million, representing a 38.5% year-over-year increase compared to $102.9 million in FY2024. Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) grew to $158.0 million with a Net Revenue Retention (NRR) rate of 124%.

## Section 2: Quarterly Revenue Breakdown
- **Q1 2025**: Revenue of $31.2 million (Gross Margin: 76.5%, Adjusted EBITDA: $4.2 million)
- **Q2 2025**: Revenue of $34.8 million (Gross Margin: 77.1%, Adjusted EBITDA: $5.1 million)
- **Q3 2025**: Revenue of $37.0 million (Gross Margin: 78.0%, Adjusted EBITDA: $6.3 million)
- **Q4 2025**: Revenue of $39.5 million (Gross Margin: 79.2%, Adjusted EBITDA: $7.8 million)
Total Full Year Adjusted EBITDA reached $23.4 million (16.4% of total revenue), up from $11.8 million (11.5% of total revenue) in FY2024.

## Section 3: Operating Expenses & R&D Investment
- **Research & Development (R&D)**: $45.6 million (32.0% of revenue), focused on multi-agent reasoning, semantic reranking models, and low-latency vector index caching.
- **Sales & Marketing (S&M)**: $48.2 million (33.8% of revenue), expanding enterprise sales teams across EMEA and APAC.
- **General & Administrative (G&A)**: $18.5 million (13.0% of revenue), including legal, compliance, and corporate overhead.
- Total operating expenses for FY2025 amounted to $112.3 million.

## Section 4: Geographic Segment Performance
- **North America (Americas)**: $88.35 million (62.0% of revenue), YoY growth of 34%.
- **Europe, Middle East, and Africa (EMEA)**: $38.48 million (27.0% of revenue), YoY growth of 46%.
- **Asia-Pacific (APAC)**: $15.67 million (11.0% of revenue), YoY growth of 52%.

## Section 5: Balance Sheet & Liquidity
As of December 31, 2025, cash, cash equivalents, and short-term marketable securities totaled $84.2 million, compared to $61.0 million at the end of FY2024. Free cash flow for the year was $19.8 million with zero outstanding long-term bank debt.
